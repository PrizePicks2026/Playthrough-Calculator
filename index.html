How this is calculated: every deposit requires a 1x playthrough before it's eligible for withdrawal, whether or not it carried a Deposit Bonus. The Progress panel above reflects your most recent deposit only. The Deposits table tracks every deposit independently: play activity (Lineup Placed and Predict Market Buy) only credits whichever deposit was the most recent one at the time — once a newer deposit comes in, later play stops crediting the older one, so an older deposit's remaining amount is never backfilled by later play. If a lineup is later refunded, the amount already credited toward playthrough is not clawed back. Any Withdrawal that happened while any deposit still had an outstanding requirement is called out separately below, since that's a case where funds went out before playthrough on a deposit was fully met. Withdrawal Refund entries are excluded since the funds never actually left the account. If a deposit was made via bank transfer, remember it's also subject to a separate 72-hour security hold regardless of playthrough status.
