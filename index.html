How this is calculated: every deposit requires a 1x playthrough before it's eligible for withdrawal, whether or not it carried a Deposit Bonus. The Progress panel above stacks every deposit into one running total owed, and play stacks against it the same way — but the moment a withdrawal actually goes through successfully (an attempted one that came back as a Withdrawal Refund doesn't count), played progress resets to zero and whatever was still owed at that moment is reduced by the withdrawal amount, then carries forward and stacks onto the next deposit. The Deposits table below is separate and always tracks every deposit independently, for transparency into each one on its own: play activity (Lineup Placed and Predict Market Buy) only credits whichever deposit was the most recent one at the time — once a newer deposit comes in, later play stops crediting the older one, so an older deposit's remaining amount is never backfilled by later play. If a lineup is later refunded, that amount is subtracted back out of the played total in both places, since the member needs to play it through again. Lineup wins do not need to be replayed — only refunded/voided lineups do. Any Withdrawal that happened while any deposit still had an outstanding requirement is called out separately below. If a deposit was made via bank transfer, remember it's also subject to a separate 72-hour security hold regardless of playthrough status.
